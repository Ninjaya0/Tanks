You will receive your order in 2 - 5 working days depending on your address.

  • The courier calls before the delivery, and if you are not available, you can always reschedule with the courier.
  • You can always check the item before you pay. In case you don’t like it, you can return it back with the courier and only pay the shipping fees.
  • In case of exchange, return the item back with the courier and pay the shipping fees, then you can place a new order through the page or the website.
  • Exchanges/returns can be made within 1 day of receiving the order.
  • Shipping costs are non-refundable.
